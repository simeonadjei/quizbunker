There's no hard limit on the number of songs you can upload — you can add as many as you like. The only constraint is 50 MB per individual song file.

One thing worth knowing: the Service Worker (which caches songs for offline playback) keeps the 20 most recently played songs cached in the browser. Songs beyond that are still streamed from the server normally when online — they just won't be available offline until they've been played and cached.
