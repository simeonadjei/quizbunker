Explaining question ordering logic

Explaining question ordering logic
Questions load by week. When a student picks a subject and week (e.g. Year 1 Mathematics → Week 3), the quiz pulls every question where week = 3, sorted by questionNumber ascending.

So a session shows:

Display label	DB questionNumber
Q1	1 (first question of that week)
Q2	2
Q3	3
…	…
Q20	20 (last question of that week)
The numbering resets to 1 for every week — it's per-week, not global. So Week 1 has its own Q1–Q20, Week 2 has its own Q1–Q20, and so on. The nav dots across the top of the quiz card correspond to those positions.

In the uploaded file, each week section like WEEK 1: NUMBER SYSTEM contains Question 1: through Question 200: (the full bank has 200 per week across DOK levels). The parser stores those numbers as-is into questionNumber, so the order the student sees matches exactly the order in the source file.
